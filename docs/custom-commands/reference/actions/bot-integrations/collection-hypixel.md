Read Hypixel player stats and whether a player is online.

### Get Hypixel player {#hypixel.get-player}

Reads the rank, level and login times of a player on Hypixel.

**Inputs**

| Name | Type | Required | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Connection | [Text](../../../value-types.md#STRING) | No | The Hypixel connection from the Integrations page that this action uses. Maximum length: 64 characters. |
| Minecraft UUID | [Text](../../../value-types.md#STRING) | Yes | The UUID of the player, with or without dashes. Get it with "Get Minecraft UUID". Maximum length: 36 characters. |

**Outputs**

| Name | Type | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Found | [Yes/No](../../../value-types.md#BOOLEAN) | Whether the player ever joined Hypixel. When not, everything else is empty. |
| Name | [Text](../../../value-types.md#STRING) | The Minecraft name Hypixel knows. May be empty. |
| Rank | [Text](../../../value-types.md#STRING) | The rank code, for example "VIP", "VIP_PLUS", "MVP", "MVP_PLUS", "MVP_PLUS_PLUS" or a staff rank. Empty without a rank. May be empty. |
| Network level | [Decimal number](../../../value-types.md#FLOAT) | The network level with its progress, for example 152.37. May be empty. |
| Karma | [Whole number](../../../value-types.md#INTEGER) | The karma of the player. May be empty. |
| Achievement points | [Whole number](../../../value-types.md#INTEGER) | The achievement points of the player. May be empty. |
| First login | [Date and time](../../../value-types.md#DATETIME) | When the player first joined. May be empty. |
| Last login | [Date and time](../../../value-types.md#DATETIME) | When the player last joined. Empty when the player hides it. May be empty. |

### Get online status {#hypixel.get-status}

Checks whether a player is on Hypixel right now and what they play.

**Inputs**

| Name | Type | Required | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Connection | [Text](../../../value-types.md#STRING) | No | The Hypixel connection from the Integrations page that this action uses. Maximum length: 64 characters. |
| Minecraft UUID | [Text](../../../value-types.md#STRING) | Yes | The UUID of the player, with or without dashes. Get it with "Get Minecraft UUID". Maximum length: 36 characters. |

**Outputs**

| Name | Type | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is online | [Yes/No](../../../value-types.md#BOOLEAN) | Whether the player is online. False also when the player hides it. |
| Game | [Text](../../../value-types.md#STRING) | The game, for example BEDWARS. May be empty. |
| Mode | [Text](../../../value-types.md#STRING) | The mode, for example LOBBY. May be empty. |
| Arena | [Text](../../../value-types.md#STRING) | The arena or world being played, when there is one. May be empty. |
